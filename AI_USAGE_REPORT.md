# AI Usage Report

**Project:** an autonomous AI purple-team platform for AI agents (B2B, bring-your-own-cloud), with a deliberately vulnerable accounts-payable multi-agent system ("LedgerPilot") as the demo target.
**Team:** Novexa · HackIndia — AI-First Startup Hackathon

## Summary

This product was built **AI-first**. AI was the primary builder across the entire lifecycle — ideation, market and competitor research, architecture, data sourcing, coding, testing, security/compliance research, UI/UX, and pitch strategy. The human founder set the direction and made the product decisions; AI did the building. We estimate **well over 80% of the code and written artifacts in this repository were generated by AI**, satisfying the hackathon's AI-first requirement.

Beyond *being built* with AI, the product *is* AI: it uses an AI red-team engine (PyRIT) and an AI guardrail classifier (LLM Guard) at runtime.

## AI across the product lifecycle

**1. Ideation & problem framing.** AI helped refine the concept from a broad idea into a focused B2B, BYOC purple-team platform for agentic AI, chose the accounts-payable target (business-email-compromise is a billions-per-year fraud class), and shaped the domain-agnostic, compliance-first positioning.

**2. Market & competitor research.** AI researched and analysed the live competitive landscape with verified sources — Straiker ($85M), Lasso ($37M), Armadin ($190M), Lakera→Check Point ($300M), Protect AI→Palo Alto ($634M), HiddenLayer ($155M) — plus market sizing, white-space analysis, and YC evaluation criteria, to sharpen differentiation (proof-based detection, verifiable evidence, CI-native, open-core).

**3. Architecture.** AI designed the whole platform: the Target adapter seam, the proof-based oracle (canary + honeytoken), the attack engine, the defender (catalog + composite guard), the verifier, the signed audit ledger, the compliance-mapping layer, and the human-oversight review queue.

**4. Data.** AI sourced a **real** invoice dataset (amazon/ConfBench, FCC public-file invoices), curated 22 usable invoices, and **verified every curated field against the source PDFs** — no fabricated data.

**5. Coding.** AI wrote essentially all the code: LedgerPilot's six-agent LangGraph pipeline, the `purpleteam` platform (discovery, adapter, oracle, attack engine on PyRIT, defender with real LLM Guard, verifier, audit ledger with Ed25519-signed hash chains, compliance mapping, human oversight, CI gate), plus a second fixture agent to prove pluggability.

**6. Testing.** AI wrote the full test suite (30+ tests, all passing) and ran live attack/defend campaigns — including the end-to-end demo where a PyRIT jailbreak that *breached* LedgerPilot is then *blocked* by LLM Guard + a tool gate, confirmed by the oracle.

**7. Security & compliance research.** AI mapped the platform to the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, DORA, ISO 42001 and OWASP LLM, and researched the applicable laws (GDPR, India DPDP, Cyber Resilience Act).

**8. UI/UX & pitch.** AI produced the dashboard directions, the typing-effect live attack→defend console, the architecture diagram, and the competitive/market positioning used in the pitch.

## Representative prompts / workflow

The build was driven by iterative founder direction and AI execution. Representative founder prompts:

- "Build an autonomous AI purple-team: attacker agent attacks, defender applies patches, a verifier confirms behaviour isn't broken, plus a hash-based audit trail. Comply with the EU AI Act."
- "The purple team should be pluggable to any multi-agent I give it — auto-discover its agents and purpose; no domain hard-coding."
- "Use a real PDF dataset so everything is real."
- "Use llm-guard correctly" / "remove the hardcoded payloads — use PyRIT's own jailbreak library."
- "Build the CI gate" / "build the signed audit ledger" / "fix the privacy — the audit stores raw payloads."
- "Compare Straiker / Lasso" / "what white space in AI is unoccupied?"

Each produced research, design, code, and tests from AI, reviewed and steered by the founder.

## Honest scope — the human role, and what is AI-built vs. designed

- **The human founder** provided all direction and product decisions, ran the terminal, supplied API keys, and contributed a *separate* earlier project ("Crucible") used **read-only as a reference** — its code was **not** copied into this repository.
- **Built and tested:** LedgerPilot, discovery, attack engine, oracle (canary + honeytoken), defender (incl. real LLM Guard), verifier, audit ledger, compliance mapping, human-oversight queue, CI gate.
- **Designed, not yet built:** the dashboard wired to live data, the fairness/bias probe, and the live deployment.

We have kept this report accurate rather than inflated: AI did the overwhelming majority of the building, the product genuinely runs, and we are transparent about what is complete versus planned.
